# Store page copy

## Title
ODE — a horror in nine stanzas

## Tagline (≤ 80 characters)
The world is a song. You are the singer who stopped.

## Short description (≤ 300 characters)
A first-person horror game drawn entirely in light-sensitive glyphs. Nine chapters, one for each
stanza of O'Shaughnessy's *Ode*. Your voice lights the dark and draws the things that listen.

## Long description
They are the music makers. Every age of the world is one of their dreams, and when they tire of it
they sing the next one over it.

You wake on a moonlit shore without a voice. It returns one measure at a time — and every measure
makes you easier to hear.

- **Nine stanzas, nine rules.** Raise a city by humming to its foundations. Slip between two ages as
  the ruins breathe. Hold your breath past a thing that hears it. Carry a flame through a house that
  is still being built. March, silent, inside a crowd that must not notice you.
- **Your voice is everything.** Hum to light the fog and find your way. Strike a chord to stagger what
  hunts you. Every sound spends Breath, and every sound is heard.
- **A world written in light.** Every frame is a real 3D scene, resolved into glyphs by material and
  surface: box-drawing stone, directional fur, Braille fog, runic light.
- **Two endings.** Sing, or stay silent.

## Content notes
Dread, pursuit, death of the player character, existential themes. No gore. No sexual content.
