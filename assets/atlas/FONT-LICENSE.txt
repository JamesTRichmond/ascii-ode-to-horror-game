DejaVu Sans Mono (https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/).

Full text: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html

Braille, sextant, box-drawing, shade-block and rune glyphs in the atlas are procedural shapes
generated by tools/bake-atlas.mjs and are part of this project.
